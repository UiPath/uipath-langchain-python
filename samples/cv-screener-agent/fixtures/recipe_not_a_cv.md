# Classic Lasagna alla Bolognese
Serves 6 · Prep 40 min · Cook 1 h 30 min

## Ingredients
- 12 fresh lasagna sheets
- 500 g minced beef, 250 g minced pork
- 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 150 ml dry red wine
- 700 ml passata, 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 50 g butter, 50 g flour, 700 ml whole milk (bechamel)
- 150 g grated Parmigiano Reggiano
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, nutmeg

## Method
1. Sweat the onion, carrot and celery in olive oil for 10 minutes, add the garlic for one more minute.
2. Add the meat and brown it well. Deglaze with the wine and let it evaporate.
3. Stir in the passata and tomato paste, season, cover and simmer for at least an hour.
4. Melt the butter, whisk in the flour, cook for two minutes, then whisk in the milk gradually. Season with salt and nutmeg.
5. In an oven dish, layer ragù, pasta, bechamel and cheese, finishing with bechamel and a generous layer of cheese.
6. Bake at 180 C for 40 minutes until golden. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting.
