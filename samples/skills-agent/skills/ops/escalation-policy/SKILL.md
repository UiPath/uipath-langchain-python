# Escalation policy

Escalate an overdue supplier invoice in this order. Do not skip a step.

1. **Confirm the age.** Count the days from the invoice due date to today. Below 31 days, stop: this policy does not apply.
2. **Check for a dispute.** If the invoice is disputed or on hold, route it to the accounts-payable lead with the dispute reason and stop.
3. **Notify the budget owner.** Send the budget owner the invoice id, supplier, amount and days overdue, and ask for payment approval within 2 business days.
4. **Escalate by age.** 31-60 days overdue: escalate to the finance manager. More than 60 days: escalate to the finance controller and flag the supplier relationship as at risk.
5. **Record the escalation.** Log the invoice id, the escalation level, who was notified and when, so the next reminder goes to the same person.
